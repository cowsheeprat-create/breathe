Controls & physics notes

Move the pointer to aim; ←→ nudge the aim (Shift = fine). ↑↓ change power, Space shoots. On touch screens drag on the table to aim.

Spin is real: every ball carries a 3-D angular velocity. Sliding friction turns slide into roll exactly (a no-spin ball ends up at 5/7 of its speed), balls collide with friction (throw) and lose speed on cushions along a speed-dependent restitution curve; the cushion nose sits above the ball centre, so topspin, backspin and side spin change the rebound angle.

The cue-ball face shows where the tip hits. The centre of the ball is a stun shot; 0.4 R above centre gives natural roll. Squirt (the cue ball leaving slightly away from the side you hit) is included.

Ideal lines are straight mirror reflections from the cushion noses. Physical lines run the full simulation of the ball for each direction with your current power and tip position, so the bounces show the effect of speed and spin. Lines stop at the first ball they would hit (ring = cue-ball position at contact).

Rules are intentionally light: turns, scratches, pocket scoring (snooker points, carom 3-cushion points, 9-ball lowest-ball-first). Group assignment and the snooker colour sequence are not enforced.
