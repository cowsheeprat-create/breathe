How to breathe

This is the most natural breath there is — it's how babies breathe, before life teaches us to breathe from the chest instead.

Inhale: your belly comes out. Exhale: your belly goes in. That's it — this simple motion sends oxygen down to the lowest, richest part of your lungs.
